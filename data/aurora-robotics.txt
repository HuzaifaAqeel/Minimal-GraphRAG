Aurora Robotics is a robotics startup. Aurora Robotics was founded by Dr. Lena Marsh in 2021. The company is based in Berlin.

Dr. Lena Marsh works at Aurora Robotics as chief executive. She previously led the perception team at Helios Dynamics.

Aurora Robotics develops the Scout quadruped robot. Scout is a four-legged inspection robot designed for industrial sites. Aurora Robotics launched Scout in 2023.

The company raised a Series A round led by Northwind Ventures in 2024. Northwind Ventures is a venture capital firm based in London.
