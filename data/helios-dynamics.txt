Helios Dynamics is an autonomous vehicle company. Helios Dynamics was founded by Dr. Lena Marsh and Tomas Reyes in 2018. The company is located in Munich.

Tomas Reyes works at Helios Dynamics as chief technology officer. Helios Dynamics develops the Wayfinder self-driving stack.

In 2022, Helios Dynamics partnered with Aurora Robotics on warehouse automation. The partnership combined Wayfinder navigation with Scout quadruped robots.
